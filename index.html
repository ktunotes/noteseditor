KTUNOTES PDF stamper

Adds the cover page, footer and watermark to your notes before you upload them.

File name

Saved files are named the same way as downloads on ktunotes.in.

Example: GXEST104_Subject-Name_Module-1_ktunotes.in.pdf

    Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded anywhere. The footer and watermark link to ktunotes.in, so readers can tap them to visit the site. Page numbers count the notes only, not the cover.
